// 04 — BIOGRAPHICAL RECORD

Rios Belmont was born on Manitoulin Island in Canada, growing up alone by the time he turned 12 after his father abandoned him and his mother died due to an undisclosed sickness.

Growing up on the streets, Rios survived by stealing whatever he could until he turned 16, at which point he lied about his age and joined the Canadian Military.

He quickly became an efficient soldier before being scouted for JTF2, where his life truly took a turn.

Upon arriving in JTF2, Rios was subjected to immense teasing during his training due to his rough upbringing. The pressure built over time until he eventually snapped during a mission in Bosnia.

During the incident, Rios severely wounded the operators he had been deployed with before subsequently aiding local Bosnian rebel forces in their efforts against Serbian forces attacking Bosnia.

He was eventually detained by United Nations Peacekeepers on the ground and transported back to Canada.

While imprisoned, Rios was found guilty and charged with Treason and Aiding & Abetting.

While imprisoned and awaiting further punishment, he was approached by an individual representing the Foundation. The representative offered Rios an opportunity to put his skills to use elsewhere.

Rios accepted the proposal.

He subsequently found himself assigned to Site-66. Now tasked with battling anomalies that he himself struggles to understand, Rios continues attempting to improve himself while finding the process increasingly difficult.
